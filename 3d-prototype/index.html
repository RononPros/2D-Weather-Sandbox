3D Weather Sandbox

The 2D sandbox idea, rebuilt as an editable 3D atmospheric volume. Paint heat, moisture and wind directly into the model, sculpt terrain, then orbit through the result.

Domain

This is an interactive educational model, not a research forecast core. Flow, terrain coupling, condensation, evaporation, precipitation and buoyancy are simulated; several expensive processes are intentionally approximated.
